About Us02 / 08

Owner first. Property always.

Poience Private Limited is building an owner-centric PropTech approach to property rental and asset management, with a focus on supporting owners beyond the initial rental.

We serve property owners, especially owners managing property from another city or country, including NRIs, with support that goes beyond finding a tenant: verification, rental coordination, maintenance and property updates.

Our vision

To build a technology-driven property management ecosystem that makes property ownership and management simpler, more transparent and better connected.

Our mission

To support property owners through rental coordination, verification processes, maintenance support and clear communication.

Chennai, with OMR as our first focus.

Our approach

The tenancy ends. Our care doesn't.

We focus on the property's long-term care, not only the act of finding a tenant.

Before
Onboarding, listing and verification of owner, property and tenant.
During
Maintenance coordination, periodic visits and property updates.
After
Exit evaluation, a condition report and care while the home is vacant.
Our future direction

Towards the connected home.

Connected property technology, smart devices and more structured maintenance coordination are part of the longer-term vision.
